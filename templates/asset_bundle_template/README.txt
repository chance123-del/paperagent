Asset bundle template

Place figures and tables in this folder, then compress its contents as ZIP.

Naming:
- Fig1.png, Fig2.tiff, or Figure3.jpg for figure placeholders [Fig1], [Fig2], [Fig3]
- Table1.xlsx, Table2.csv, or Table3.tsv for table placeholders [Table1], [Table2], [Table3]

Use one matching asset per placeholder. Do not include executable files.
