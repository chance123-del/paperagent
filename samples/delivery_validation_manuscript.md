# Retrieval-Assisted Revision for Structured Academic Writing

## Abstract

This synthetic manuscript is an acceptance-test input for PaperFormat Agent. It evaluates whether a structured revision workflow can preserve heading hierarchy, tables, citations, and LaTeX-sensitive symbols during conversion. The data are illustrative only.

Keywords: academic writing, LaTeX conversion, validation

## Introduction

Academic writing workflows often require the same source to satisfy multiple format constraints. A small agent-assisted pipeline can make these transformations traceable while retaining editable source material [1].
